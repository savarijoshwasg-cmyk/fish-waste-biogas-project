🌱 Project Overview

This project focuses on developing a sustainable biogas production and purification system using biodegradable fish waste and cow dung through anaerobic digestion.

The proposed system integrates a combined biochar–zeolite treatment approach to support biogas purification and promote sustainable waste-to-energy conversion.

🎯 Objectives

* To utilize fish waste for renewable biogas production.
* To develop a biogas generation and collection prototype.
* To incorporate biochar and zeolite for biogas treatment.
* To explore sustainable organic waste management.
* To promote renewable energy and environmental sustainability.

⚙️ Key Components

* Fish waste
* Cow dung
* Anaerobic digestion system
* Biochar
* Zeolite
* Combined biochar–zeolite capsule
* Biogas collection system
* Gas purification components

🔬 Project Methodology

1. Collection and preparation of biodegradable feedstock.
2. Preparation of the biochar and zeolite materials.
3. Development of the biogas production prototype.
4. Anaerobic digestion of the feedstock.
5. Collection of the generated biogas.
6. Integration of the biochar–zeolite treatment system.
7. Experimental observation and evaluation.

💡 Innovation

The project explores the integration of fish-waste-based biogas production with a combined biochar–zeolite treatment system as an approach to sustainable renewable energy generation.
